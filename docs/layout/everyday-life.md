# Scenario Layout Analysis: Everyday Life & Academic Laboratory

## 1. Experiential Goals
Ordinary contemporary life emphasizes academic deadlines, peer conversations, email notifications, grant proposals, social calendar events, and reputational standings.

## 2. Spatial Composition
* **Primary Stage (60%)**: Interactive Weekly Schedule & Research Workbench—active experiment assays, pending paper drafts, and lecture timetables.
* **Satellite Rail (30%)**: Lab Roster & Peer Network—co-author relations, advisor feedback, grant review committee notes.
* **Ambient Monitor (10%)**: Grant Funding Remaining, Academic Stress, Tenure Clock.
* **Action Dock**: Everyday life actions ("Submit paper revision to IEEE", "Confront lab partner regarding authorship", "Attend departmental seminar").
