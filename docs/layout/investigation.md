# Scenario Layout Analysis: Forensic Mystery & Investigation

## 1. Experiential Goals
Mystery solving requires cross-referencing disparate clues, analyzing chemical/ballistic reports, testing alibis, pinning photos to an evidence board, and tracing motive threads.

## 2. Spatial Composition
* **Primary Stage (65%)**: Interactive Evidence Corkboard with pinned forensic exhibits, witness testimony cards, and colored connection yarn.
* **Satellite Rail (25%)**: Suspect Interrogation Roster & Exhibit Inspector—deep dive into fingerprints, handwriting samples, or medical examiner findings.
* **Ambient Monitor (10%)**: Case Solvability Gauge, Police Deadline Clock.
* **Action Dock**: Deductive actions ("Run chemical assay on powder", "Interrogate groundskeeper on timeline gap", "Present murder weapon to suspect").
