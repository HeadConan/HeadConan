# Scenario Layout Analysis: Geopolitical Strategy & Realm Management

## 1. Experiential Goals
High-level strategy centers faction influence, macro-economic ledgers, military garrison deployments, succession diplomacy, and legislative decrees.

## 2. Spatial Composition
* **Primary Stage (60%)**: Geopolitical Theater & Faction Tensor Matrix—showing Great House power scores, treaty obligations, and border garrisons.
* **Satellite Rail (30%)**: Imperial Cabinet / Faction Dossier—diplomatic leverage, crown debt ledger, and covert blackmail files.
* **Ambient Monitor (10%)**: Imperial Stability, Realm Treasury, Food Reserves.
* **Action Dock**: Statecraft directives ("Impose grain embargo", "Convene Small Council", "Authorize clandestine assassination").
