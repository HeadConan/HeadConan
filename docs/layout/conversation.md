# Scenario Layout Analysis: Social Conversation & Interrogation

## 1. Experiential Goals
Social interactions require deep focus on the speaker's emotional state, body language, unspoken subtext, hidden allegiances, and conversational momentum.

## 2. Spatial Composition
* **Primary Stage (65%)**: Dialogue stage rendering speaker portraits, dynamic dialogue bubbles, subtext whispers, and speech option trees.
* **Satellite Rail (25%)**: Target NPC Dossier—loyalty rating, known secrets, recent psychological reactions, and relationship with the player.
* **Ambient Monitor (10%)**: Interpersonal rapport / tension meter, social etiquette stakes.
* **Action Dock**: Quick conversation prompts ("Praise their loyalty", "Confront with forged letter", "Change subject quietly").

## 3. Epistemic Behavior
The player only hears outward speech; psychic abilities (e.g. Anya) or high perception skills unlock a "Subtext Layer" beneath the spoken quote.
