# The 12 Golden Worlds Portfolio

## 1. Executive Selection Strategy

The **12 Golden Worlds** represent HeadConan's flagship portfolio. They have been curated not merely by commercial popularity, but to maximize **orthogonal diversity** across seven key product vectors:

1. **User Fantasy Variety**: Identity, Power, Social belonging, Romance/Camouflage, Exploration, Mystery, System building, and Statecraft.
2. **Social Structure Diversity**: Hierarchical Empires, Domestic Nuclear Deceptions, Academic Cliques, Anarchic Frontiers, High-Finance Boardrooms, and Claustrophobic Bureaucracies.
3. **Player Agency Spectrum**: From micro-tactical dialogue negotiations to macro-historical senate decrees.
4. **World Complexity**: Highly mathematical economies to psychological perception mechanics.
5. **Interaction Style**: Inquest & Deduction, Negotiation & Intrigue, Resource Management & Expeditions, Spatial Navigation.
6. **Emotional Tone**: Melancholic Wonder, High-Stakes Paranoia, Academic Cozy Nostalgia, Razor-Sharp Satire, and Tense Survival.
7. **Generative UI Archetypes**: Dossiers, Evidence Graphs, Senate Slates, Star Maps, Expediter Rails, Divergence Meters.

## 2. Detailed Profiles of the 12 Golden Worlds

---

### World 1: SPY × FAMILY (Cold War Berlint)
- **Category**: Fictional IP (`licensed_required`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Relationship & Social Camouflage (Secondary: Identity / Espionage)
- **Core Premise**: Ostania and Westalis teeter on the brink of war. You navigate an undercover double life where every family member conceals an explosive secret behind a facade of wholesome domestic normalcy.
- **Inhabited Role**: Master Spy / Secret Assassin / Undercover Diplomat / Eden Academy Teacher.
- **Core Loop**: Receive handler intelligence briefing → Plan domestic cover scenario → Attend elite Eden Academy gala → Perform stealth extraction/interrogation → Protect domestic cover during dinner.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Cover Integrity Meter* (0–100% Suspicion)
  - *Dual Dossier Slate* (Public Persona vs. Covert Operative Identity)
  - *Eden Stella / Tonitrus Star Gauge*
  - *Domestic Family Table Dialogue Radar*
- **Why Inhabited**: Reading the manga is delightful; *inhabiting* the world forces the player to balance conflicting conversational objectives in real-time without blowing their cover.

### World 2: Hogwarts School of Witchcraft & Wizardry (Original Student Run)
- **Category**: Fictional IP (`licensed_required`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Social & Academic Magic Mastery (Secondary: Mystery)
- **Core Premise**: September 1st, 1991. You are an original first-year student sorted into your house. The castle is brimming with secret passages, ancient rivalries, dangerous forbidden corridors, and midnight dueling clubs.
- **Inhabited Role**: Ravenclaw/Slytherin/Gryffindor/Hufflepuff Student, Hogwarts Professor, Auror Investigator.
- **Core Loop**: Attend lectures (Potions, Transfiguration) → Manage house rivalries and curfew → Sneak into the Restricted Section → Uncover hidden castle artifacts → Compete in House Cup.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Castle Floor Blueprint & Secret Passages*
  - *Spellbook & Potion Cauldron Workbench*
  - *Four-House Hourglass Score Tally*
  - *Class Schedule & Midnight Curfew Clock*
- **Why Inhabited**: Transcends Harry's fixed canon into an authentic open-ended magical academic life simulator.

### World 3: Victorian Sherlock London (Baker Street 1895)
- **Category**: Fictional / Literature (`public_domain`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Mystery & Forensic Deduction (Secondary: Identity)
- **Core Premise**: Gaslit Victorian London where Scotland Yard is baffled by impossible crimes, aristocratic blackmail, and covert underworld syndicates operating in the East End docks.
- **Inhabited Role**: Independent Consulting Detective, Scotland Yard Inspector, Underground Informant.
- **Core Loop**: Inspect crime scene for subtle physical anomalies → Interview witnesses/suspects → Categorize evidence and refute alibis → Construct inductive theory → Confront the perpetrator.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Forensic Evidence Pinboard with Yarn Links*
  - *Witness Alibi Consistency Matrix*
  - *Victorian London Gazette Dispatch*
  - *Magnifying Anomaly Analyzer*
- **Why Inhabited**: Allows genuine deduction from incomplete clues rather than watching Sherlock monologue the solution.

### World 4: Game of Thrones (Westeros War of the Five Kings)
- **Category**: Fictional IP (`licensed_required`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Political Intrigue & Asymmetric Statecraft (Secondary: Survival)
- **Core Premise**: The Iron Throne is contested. Seven great houses mobilize armies, forge marriage pacts, and deploy assassins as winter looms and food stockpiles dwindle.
- **Inhabited Role**: Lord of a Minor Vassal House, King's Hand, Master of Whisperers, Braavosi Iron Banker.
- **Core Loop**: Review raven messages → Balance grain and treasury reserves → Maneuver levy battalions on the realm map → Conduct tense backroom marriage negotiations → Survive council betrayal.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Westeros Strategic SVG Realm Map with Army Tokens*
  - *Great House Stance & Marriage Alliance Graph*
  - *Raven Scroll Inbox & Decryption Table*
  - *Grain Stockpile & Treasury Ledger*
- **Why Inhabited**: The player experiences the brutal reality of political trade-offs where honor and survival are mutually exclusive.

### World 6: Outer Wilds Solar Loop

- **Primary Fantasy**: Exploration & Cosmic Epiphany (Secondary: Mystery & Causality)

- **Core Loop**: Launch wooden spaceship → Scan radio frequencies with Signalscope → Translate ancient Nomai wall texts → Discover quantum mechanics rules → Race against supernova clock.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Planetary Orbital Radar & Gravitational Vectors*
  - *Interactive Ship Log Knowledge-Web*
  - *Signalscope Audio Frequency Tuner*
  - *Nomai Spiral Text Translator Slate*
- **Why Inhabited**: Progression is gated entirely by player curiosity and knowledge, creating pure epistemic wonder.

### World 7: Ancient Rome (Late Republic Senate 63 BCE)
- **Category**: Historical World (`historical_real_world`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Macro Statecraft & Forensic Rhetoric (Secondary: Political Intrigue)
- **Core Premise**: Rome teeters between Republican democracy and autocratic military dictatorship during the Catiline Conspiracy. Cicero, Caesar, and Cato clash in the Curia.
- **Inhabited Role**: Senatorial Consul, Plebeian Tribune, Provincial Governor, Patrician Financier.
- **Core Loop**: Draft legislative motions → Bribe or persuade key voting blocs → Deliver impassioned speeches on the Rostra → Deploy urban mobs / Praetorian guards → Manage grain dole fleets.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Curia Senate Chamber Voting Tally (Optimates vs. Populares)*
  - *Tribune Veto Button & Oratorical Influence Dial*
  - *Roman Forum Map & Mob Sentiment Thermometer*
  - *Mediterranean Grain Fleet Supply Tracker*
- **Why Inhabited**: Zero IP constraints; pure historical consequence where player rhetoric directly reshapes Western civilization.

### World 8: Heian Kyoto Imperial Court (The Tale of Genji Era, 1000 CE)
- **Category**: Historical World (`historical_real_world`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Relationship, Social Etiquette & Subtle Intrigue (Secondary: Identity)
- **Core Premise**: The cloistered aristocracy of Heian-kyō (Kyoto) lives in an aesthetic world governed by poetry exchange, seasonal sensitivity (mono no aware), fabric color matching, and behind-the-screen court romance.
- **Inhabited Role**: Lady-in-Waiting, Court Poet, Fujiwara Regent, Buddhist Scholar-Monk.
- **Core Loop**: Compose seasonal Waka poetry with subtle double-meanings → Exchange scented calligraphy scrolls → Attend the Cherry Blossom Festival → Influence imperial succession quietly.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Calligraphy & Waka Poetry Composition Slate*
  - *Silk Layer Color Harmony Meter (Jūnihitoe)*
  - *Screen of Separation (Misu) Visibility Slider*
  - *Court Rank (Itai) & Imperial Favor Ladder*
- **Why Inhabited**: Offers a radically different emotional frequency from Western combat fantasy—focused on subtlety, longing, and aesthetic etiquette.

### World 9: Modern Elite University (Ivy League Semester)
- **Category**: Real-World / Life World (`historical_real_world`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Life, Ambition & Social Web (Secondary: Identity / Academic)
- **Core Premise**: An intense autumn semester at a high-pressure university. Academic rivalries, secret societies, research breakthroughs, dorm tensions, and career existentialism intersect.
- **Inhabited Role**: Undergraduate Student, Tenured Faculty Advisor, Dean of Admissions, Secret Society President.
- **Core Loop**: Balance weekly course syllabus vs. lab research → Attend campus networking/social mixers → Navigate dining hall clique dynamics → Publish thesis / Secure internship.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Weekly Schedule & Sleep/Energy Barometer*
  - *Social Clique Affinity Graph (Professors, Peers, Crushes)*
  - *Academic GPA & Research Breakthrough Gauge*
  - *Personal Laptop / Email Inbox & Notes App*
- **Why Inhabited**: Highly relatable everyday human stakes that resonate with immense emotional intimacy without requiring fantasy dragons.

  - *Product Kanban Sprint Board*

### World 11: Disco Elysium (Martinaise Coastal Slum)
- **Category**: Fictional Gaming (`licensed_required`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Internal Psychology & World Lore (Secondary: Identity & Mystery)
- **Core Premise**: A war-scarred coastal district in the city of Revachol. You wake up with catastrophic amnesia while a corpse hangs behind the hostel. Your own 24 psychological traits debate each other in your head.
- **Inhabited Role**: Amnesiac Detective, Hardcore Moralist Inspector, Union Dockworker Organizer.
- **Core Loop**: Dialogue with internal personality voices (Inland Empire, Logic, Shivers) → Interrogate eccentric locals → Internalize thoughts in the Thought Cabinet → Piece together political history.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Psychological Trait Debate Wheel (24 Voices)*
  - *Thought Cabinet Mental Project Builder*
  - *Morale & Health Pip Trackers*
  - *Political Compass Alignment Dial*
- **Why Inhabited**: The ultimate benchmark for literary-grade interactive dialogue and internal psychological roleplaying.

### World 12: The Dystopian Border Checkpoint (Papers, Please Archetype)
- **Category**: Original Archetypal World (`public_domain` / `original_archetype`)
- **Primary Fantasy**: Survival, Moral Dilemma & Bureaucratic Power (Secondary: Political)
- **Core Premise**: You are an inspector at the sole border gate of a totalitarian regime. A new regulation arrives every morning. Contraband smugglers, political dissidents, and desperate families present forged papers.
- **Inhabited Role**: Chief Border Inspector, Resistance Courier, Ministry Liaison.
- **Core Loop**: Review contradictory rulebook → Cross-examine passports and entry permits → Stamp APPROVE or DENY → Take bribes or aid the resistance → Pay nightly heating/food bills for family.
- **Generative UI Affordance**:
  - *Inspector Desk with Moveable Documents & Stampers*
  - *Discrepancy Highlight Scanner*
  - *Rulebook Index Binder*
  - *Daily Family Survival Expense Ledger*
- **Why Inhabited**: Extreme mechanical clarity; demonstrates how physical manipulation of UI documents produces unforgettable moral tension.
