# Product Vision & Hypotheses — HeadConan

## 1. Executive Summary

HeadConan tests a fundamental thesis:
> **Can an AI transform a user's freeform imagination into an interactive environment whose interface dynamically evolves with that imagination?**

## 2. Core Archetypes

### A. Fictional Authoritarian Empire ("Become a Ruler")
- **Premise**: You are the newly appointed ruler of an empire showing subtle fractures.
- **Key Modalities**: Interactive strategic SVG map (Capital, Border, Headquarters, Parliament), Cabinet Dossiers (Chancellor, Defense Minister, Intelligence Director), Faction Influence meters (Military, Parliament, Oligarchs, Opposition), Urgent Intelligence Memos, Live Ticker.

### B. University Life ("A Semester to Remember")
- **Premise**: An intense semester with packed academic schedules, shifting social cliques, dorm tensions, and romantic possibilities.
- **Key Modalities**: Campus Map, Social Dynamics Graph, Day Schedule Timeline, Personal Diary / Notes, Relationship Affection meters.

### C. The Last City ("Humanity's Redoubt")
- **Premise**: The final citadel enclosed by unmapped anomaly sectors and dwindling energy grids.
- **Key Modalities**: Sector Grid Map, Power Core Diagnostics, Expedition Log, Council Factions.

## 3. Product Loop

```
User Prompt ("I want to...")
  ↓
World Genesis Animation (Cinematic Genesis Flow)
  ↓
Generative Interface (Custom UI Composition)
  ↓
Natural Language Interaction ("Investigate the Chancellor's bank transfers")
  ↓
World State Update & Narrative Feed
  ↓
Dynamic UI Adaptation
```
